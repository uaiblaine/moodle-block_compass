What rises in the teaching view

  • A session the teacher runs (an event with a start: Google Meet, BigBlueButton, attendance session): the full ladder, “Agora” included, with Entrar.
  • Grading due today (assign’s gradingdue, or the Feedback tracker’s ledger where it covers the course): the full ladder, with Avaliar and the number waiting.
  • The class’s deadline (the students’ due date): listed in time order but capped at “soon”, never amber, with Ver.

Data this needs

  • “8 a avaliar” is the grading count the strip already computes (core’s rule, or the ledger).
  • optional “12 de 30 enviaram” is new: one count per class deadline in the horizon, two at most. Without it the row reads “prazo da turma” only.
  • “Plantão de dúvidas” needs the event’s duration to know when “Agora” ends.
