What never enters

  • An item whose action core has removed: submitted, attempted, closed.
  • An event that ended, and every “done” item.
  • Anything past the horizon, even today’s (it stays in the count).
  • Tomorrow’s first hours count only while inside the horizon: at 22:00, a 01:00 deadline is “amanhã 01:00 · em 3 h”.

Time, words and screen readers

  • Always the clock time and the countdown together; never a countdown alone.
  • “fecha”, “começa”, “termina” follow the kind; the row says it in its accessible name.
  • The countdown ticks once a minute in the browser; no request per tick.
  • A polite live region speaks only when a level rises, never each minute.

Weight

  • At most two rows; a third and later go to “Mais N hoje”.
  • Hidden by the reader until the level rises (soon → imminent → now).
  • Amber means “under an hour”, red stays for late: the ladder never borrows the danger colour.
  • Reduced motion: the live dot stops pulsing.
