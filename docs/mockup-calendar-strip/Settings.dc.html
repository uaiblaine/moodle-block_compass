Compass

Calendar strip

A row of days above the first tier, from a few days ago to the end of next month, weighed by what each day holds. Pointing at a day lists its items with the actions the Timeline offers.

block_compass | calendar_enabled
When off, the strip costs nothing: no request, no query.
block_compass | calendar_where
Default: Dashboard and the Compass page
The surfaces that show the calendar strip.
Calendar sources
block_compass | calendar_sources
Default: Deadlines and grading work, Course events, and the events of its activities, Group events, Site events
What the strip counts and a day lists. Deadlines are the activities with something to do, and for a teacher the submissions waiting to be graded.
block_compass | calendar_style
Default: Dots
How each day is drawn. The ring needs more room per day, so it shows fewer days at once.
block_compass | calendar_relevance
The starting value of each user's own switch. On, graded work, starred courses and work due today or tomorrow weigh more; off, a day's colour follows only how many items it holds.
Weekends, holidays and recesses
no setting of its own (ADR-014 amendment 10)
Without block_feedback_tracker, the weekend is core's calendar_weekend (Site administration / Appearance / Calendar), Saturday and Sunday when never saved. With block_feedback_tracker installed, its academic calendar is the truth: its weekend mask and its holidays, recesses and closed days, read in one statement when the strip is built. Never both.
