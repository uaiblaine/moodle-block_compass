Compass

Calendar strip

A row of days above the attention tier. The strip costs one request when the block loads; a day's details load only when someone points at it.

block_compass | calendar_enabled
Off by default. Turned off, the block makes no calendar request at all.
Default: No
block_compass | calendar_where
The strip runs from the days before today that fill the row to the end of next month. A wider range is the calendar's job, linked from the strip.
Default: Dashboard and the block's page
What the strip counts
block_compass | calendar_sources
Activity deadlines are the ones the Timeline block shows, with the same action buttons.
block_compass | calendar_style
Dots under each day, segments around it, or bars above it. Ring shows 15 days where dots show 21; pulse suits a narrow region.
Default: Dots
block_compass | calendar_relevance
Graded activities weigh more, most of all when due today or tomorrow; so do starred and recently opened courses and completion criteria. Late work still open weighs little. Learners can switch to a plain count, and the strip remembers their choice.
Default: Yes
