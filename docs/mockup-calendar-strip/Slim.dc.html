ADR-014 follow-up · design proposal · not built

The calendar strip, closed: counts by period on one line

The maintainer's choice (option B): the slim strip is the strip's closed state, as block_feedback_tracker's slim hero is its dashboard's. Closed, the strip is one line: its toggle, then how many items fall late, today, tomorrow, this week and later, with no day columns. Opening it shows the row of days as built. The choice is the viewer's and is remembered in block_compass_calendar; the first paint honours it. Closed never asks get_strip or get_day: the counts come from a small service that answers from the strip's cache when it is warm and from a counts-only pass when it is not (the "Where the counts come from" panel).

Decided by the maintainer, 2026-10-14: the counts come from get_strip_counts; a cold count omits Late until the strip has been opened that day; a viewer who never chose starts closed; the closed line shows plain counts per period, with no kind shapes and no "up to" (the open strip keeps both as built); "N submissions waiting to grade" shows only with block_feedback_tracker installed; no needle and no days-off note on the closed line.

  • 1. Recommended, interactive. The Dashboard block, closed and open, Studying and Teaching, with a request log. Tweaks: dark, latency, the cache's state, a failing open, and the preference at page load.
  • 2. Transitions. First paint closed and open, opening, a failed open with its retry, closing again.
  • 3. Where the counts come from. Three sources, their cost and how stale each can be; S2 decided.
  • 4. States and what a dateless line drops.
  • 5. Layouts considered for the line, and option A (slim as a style of its own) kept as the alternative.
  • 6. Settings, 7. phone, 8. Teaching with and without block_feedback_tracker (dark), 9. anatomy and accessibility.

3 · Where the counts come from

Closed must cost less than a strip build: answer from the cache, else count only the future

A strip build is ≤ 61 reads (62 with block_feedback_tracker), and 49 of E2's 60 are the past part through core's vault, the only way to know "late, still open" exactly (ADR-014 decision 5). The cache entry lives per sign-in and day, so the first page after signing in is always cold. Three sources were weighed; S2 was decided.

Option A · the alternative, not chosen

Slim as a style of its own

The admin picks "Counts by period" as a fourth strip style beside dots, ring and pulse. Closed is then a bare header with no numbers and no request; open is either the line or the row, never both.

Closed: "▸ Calendar", nothing else. 0 requests.
Open, style "Counts": the line above. get_strip, the full build.
Open, dots / ring / pulse: the row. get_strip.

Cheaper to reason about (closed is free, no new service) but the counts cost a full build and the viewer cannot move between summary and detail: that is the admin's choice. B gives the viewer both and makes the summary the cheap state.

9 · Anatomy and accessibility

One heading, one disclosure, a list of buttons

1Calendar 2
  • {{b.label}}{{b.shown}}
    •
3
4StudyingTeaching 5
  • 1 Heading and disclosure. The toggle is a button inside the strip's heading (level from heading.ts, 4/3/2 by headinglevel), the WAI disclosure pattern: aria-expanded and aria-controls naming the row's region. Its name is "Calendar"; the state is the attribute, never the text.
  • 2 The periods are a list of buttons (ul named "Your calendar, by period"), each named by one sentence: "This week: 8 items. Opens the strip on these days." The label and number inside are aria-hidden, as the row's dots are.
  • 3 Plain counts, spoken as drawn. No shapes and no "up to" on the closed line, in the picture or in the sentence (the maintainer). The open strip keeps both as built, cal_upto included (ADR-014 amendment 9).
  • 4 The view switch keeps role="group" and aria-pressed; switching while closed costs no request (the counts carry both views, as the strip payload does).
  • 5 "Open the calendar" becomes an icon link named by aria-label and title, 40 px, the control border (4.8:1).
  • · Live region, polite, keyed on a counter: once when the counts arrive ("Calendar: 2 late, still open, 3 today, 1 tomorrow, 8 this week, 18 later"), never on a later re-render. Opening the strip announces nothing extra: the region and its skeleton carry aria-busy; a failure is the RetryNotice's role="alert".
  • · Focus. Toggling keeps focus on the toggle. A period button opens the strip and moves focus to its first day (or the pinned day card for Today and Tomorrow). When a retry succeeds and the notice leaves, focus goes to the toggle: RetryNotice's fallback today is .compass-reload, which the no-reload rule removes, so its target must change.
  • · Motion and colour. No height animation on open or close; the shimmer stops under reduced motion. Late's number is the danger colour (5.29:1 on white, 6.69:1 on the dark surface) with its hollow diamond, so nothing depends on hue. Labels are sentence case, never uppercase (feedback_tracker's eyebrows are; Compass's rule wins).
